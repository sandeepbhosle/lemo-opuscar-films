# mSUBS ad copy

Copy for three campaign stories, each matching a video in `out/video/`. Facts come from [infoesearch.com/msubs](https://infoesearch.com/msubs) and [msubs.in](https://msubs.in) (read 26 Nov 2026).

| Story | Video files | Core message |
|---|---|---|
| **File Subs** | `file-*.mp4` | Drop any video or audio file and get timed subtitles in 100+ languages, exported as SRT, VTT, SCC, TTML or burned-in. |
| **Live Subs** | `live-*.mp4` | Stream from OBS, vMix, Larix, RTMP or HLS; captions appear within seconds and are translated live. |
| **Human edit** | `edit-*.mp4` | AI does 90%; editors (yours or Infoesearch's native linguists) fix names, terms and timing. |

Positioning line, from the site: **AI for speed. Native linguists for trust.**
Default CTA: **Start free trial** at **msubs.in** (every new account gets 30 free minutes for 14 days). Alternatives the site uses: "Request a free sample" (one business day turnaround) and "Book a demo".

## Facts you can use

- 100+ languages for subtitles and captions; msubs.in says timed captions in 103 languages, translated into 119.
- Export formats: SRT, VTT, SCC, TTML, burned-in. Self-serve plans list SRT, VTT and TXT downloads plus an online subtitle editor.
- Live sources: OBS, vMix, Larix, Restream, RTMP, HLS. Output to YouTube, Facebook, any RTMP server, or YouTube's CC button.
- Live editor review: each line can be held for up to 30 seconds so an editor can fix names and terms before it airs; translations follow the correction.
- Live language switching: when a stream changes language (an ad, a guest, a song), captions follow the language being spoken.
- 15M+ caption minutes delivered. The site marks this as a verified figure.
- Compliance: FCC, CVAA, ADA, Section 508, AODA, WCAG 2.0 AA. Infoesearch is ISO 27001 certified.
- Plans (INR): Starter ₹999/month (120 min), Pro ₹2,999/month (600 min), Business ₹7,999/month (2,000 min).

**Don't use** the case-study numbers on the mSUBS page (30 languages in 72 hours, 100% FCC compliance, 6× faster) or the "Δ 0.8s" latency. The page itself marks them as placeholder or illustrative. For live speed, say "within seconds".

The **90%** figure is your framing of how much of the work the AI does; the website doesn't state it. Keep it as a description of the workflow, not a measured accuracy claim.
