# mSubs ad copy

Copy for campaigns around three stories, each matching a video in `out/video/`:

| Story | Video files | Core message |
|---|---|---|
| **File Subs** | `file-*.mp4` | Upload any video and get timed subtitles, ready to export. |
| **Live Subs** | `live-*.mp4` | Live captions for streams, webinars and events. |
| **Human edit** | `edit-*.mp4` | AI does 90%; your editors fix the flagged words and approve. |

Positioning line: **AI speed. Human accuracy.**

> Before launch, check every claim against the product (see "Claims to verify" in `README.md`). Export formats, supported languages and the CTA ("Book a demo") are placeholders.

## YouTube / CTV: 15s spots (sound-off friendly)

The videos are silent and self-explanatory. If you add a voiceover, these scripts fit the timing:

**File Subs (15s)**
- 0–2s: "Drop a video…"
- 2–7s: "mSubs transcribes it, syncs every timecode and splits it into readable lines."
- 7–11s: "Export subtitles in minutes, not hours."
- 11–15s: "mSubs by Infoesearch. Book a demo."

**Live Subs (15s)**
- 0–2s: "Going live?"
- 2–11s: "mSubs captions every word the moment it's spoken, for webinars, events and broadcasts."
- 11–15s: "mSubs Live Subs, by Infoesearch."

**Human edit (15s)**
- 0–2s: "AI does ninety percent."
- 2–9s: "mSubs flags the words it isn't sure of: names, brand terms, speakers. Your editor fixes them in a click."
- 9–11s: "Approve and export."
- 11–15s: "AI speed. Human accuracy. mSubs by Infoesearch."

**6s bumper (cut from `edit-landscape.mp4` 4s–10s, then end card):** "AI does 90%. Your team makes it 100%. mSubs."

## Suggested campaign structure

| Funnel stage | Story | Placement | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | File Subs, Live Subs | Reels/Stories, YouTube in-stream | Video views, reach |
| Consideration | Human edit | LinkedIn feed, Meta feed | Landing-page visits |
| Conversion | Human edit end card + retargeting | LinkedIn lead gen, Google Search | Demo bookings |

Retarget people who watched 50%+ of a File Subs or Live Subs video with the Human edit ad. It answers the main objection to AI subtitles: "will it get our names and terms right?"
